# AttributeCardExtension – Premium Attribute Upgrade Mod

**Elevate your gameplay with over 80 meticulously crafted attribute cards** inspired by League of Legends’ RPG mechanics. Each card enhances one of 13 core stats across six rarity tiers (Common → Mythic), offering dynamic scaling and meaningful progression.

### Key Highlights
- **13 Custom Stats** – Max Health, Attack Damage (AD), Ability Power (AP), Attack Speed (AS), Critical Strike Chance, Armor, Magic Resist (MR), Life Steal, Omnivamp, Movement Speed (MS), HP Regen, Ability Haste, Bonus XP.
- **Six Rarity Levels** – From Common to Mythic, each providing increasingly potent bonuses.
- **Integrated RPG Scaling** – Earn vanilla XP, level up, and choose from three random cards on each level‑up event.
- **Instant Access** – Press **L** to view a sleek, scrollable overlay of your active buffs.
- **Zero‑Code Integration** – Install alongside **CardEngine** and enjoy the full suite with a single mod‑load.

*Requires the lightweight **CardEngine** library.*

Experience a polished, balanced upgrade system that feels both familiar and fresh—perfect for modders and players seeking depth without complexity.
